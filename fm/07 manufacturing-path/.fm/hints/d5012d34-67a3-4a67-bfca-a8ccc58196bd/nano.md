A clean flat-design educational infographic titled "The Quality Feedback Loop" with subtitle "Failed checks trace back to the machine". Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape, generous white space, light neutral background.

LAYOUT: One large closed circular loop dominates the center of the canvas, drawn as a wide horizontal oval track. Four rounded rectangular entity cards sit evenly along the TOP arc of the oval, read left to right. A single thick curved RETURN ARROW sweeps along the BOTTOM arc, flowing right to left, closing the ring. The eye travels left-to-right across the top, then sweeps back along the bottom to the start, making the continuous-improvement cycle obvious.

TOP ARC — forward production flow, four cards connected left to right by thick blue arrows pointing right:
1. Card labeled "Machine" with a small gear icon, small caption under it: "factory equipment"
2. Card labeled "Work-Order" with a clipboard icon, caption: "production job"
3. Card labeled "Part" with a cube icon, caption: "manufactured item"
4. Card labeled "Quality-Check" with a magnifying-glass icon, caption: "inspection result"
A small blue banner above these four cards reads "FORWARD PRODUCTION FLOW".

Small relationship labels sit on each blue arrow between the cards, in tiny uppercase text: "assigned_to" between Machine and Work-Order, "produces" between Work-Order and Part, "inspects" between Part and Quality-Check.

BOTTOM ARC — the return path: one bold RED curved arrow starts at the Quality-Check card on the right, sweeps down and left along the bottom of the oval, and points back into the Machine card on the left. A red pill-shaped label sits on this arrow reading "failed check traces back". Directly under the Quality-Check card, a small red tag reads "passed = false" as the trigger. Three small red waypoint dots on the return arc are labeled, right to left: "Part", "Work-Order", "Machine".

CENTER OF THE LOOP: inside the oval, a circular-arrows recycle symbol with the words "CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT" beneath it, and two tiny outcome chips: "machine maintenance" and "work-order re-planning".

LEFT SIDE, outside the ring: a small separate card labeled "Sensor" with a signal-wave icon, connected by a thin dashed gray arrow pointing to the Machine card, labeled "monitors".

STYLE: clean flat design, educational infographic, minimal vector illustration, soft rounded corners, subtle drop shadows, limited palette of blue for forward flow, red for the failure return path, gray for supporting elements. Crisp sans-serif typography. All labels short, three to five words maximum. No photorealism, no clutter, no gradients on text, high legibility.
